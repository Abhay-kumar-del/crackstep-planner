How to use CrackStep

A quick guide to planning, tracking real study time and reading your weekly report.

1. Make and follow your daily plan
  1. Enter the total time you can genuinely study today. Breaks are not included in this time.
  2. Choose the subjects you want to study and select a priority that matches your goal for today.
  3. Tap Generate my plan. Work through the rounds in order, attempt questions before checking solutions, and tick a task only when you finish it.
  4. The progress bar shows completed tasks divided by all tasks. It tracks checklist completion—not how many minutes you actually studied.
2. How to use the study timer
  1. Scroll to Weekly subject-hour tracker and choose the subject you are about to study.
  2. Tap Start study timer when you begin focused study. Keep this page open while studying.
  3. When you finish or switch subjects, tap Stop & save session. The elapsed time is saved under the selected subject.
  4. For a break, stop the timer first. Start a new session when study resumes. Do not run one session across different subjects.

The timer uses elapsed clock time. If you forget to stop it, the session can overcount. It does not automatically pause or detect whether you are studying.

3. How to read the weekly report
  1. The report totals saved timer sessions from the last 7 days, grouped by subject.
  2. Use it to see where your actual study time went—not just what you planned.
  3. Compare time with results: if a subject takes many hours but mock accuracy stays low, review your method and mistakes.
  4. At the end of the week, set next week's priorities based on weak topics, accuracy and unfinished revision.
4. Share, download and save your work
  • Share plan sends or copies a text snapshot of your current plan. It does not share your ticking progress live.
  • Download plan saves a text file. Print / Save PDF opens your browser's print dialog.
  • Plan settings and checklist progress are stored in this browser on this device. Weekly timer sessions are stored separately in the same browser.
5. Important limits and reset advice
  • This is a free, local planner: there is no account, cloud backup or automatic sync between devices/browsers.
  • Reset progress unticks the current plan; it does not erase your weekly timer history.
  • Clear weekly history deletes recorded timer sessions. Use it only if you really want to erase those records.
  • For a fair report, use the timer consistently and stop each session when you finish.
